Security and data protection

How Codexta protects merchant and shopper data: encryption, backups, separation of test and production, limits on staff access, and how this maps to Shopify's protected customer data requirements. Draft for review. Last updated 14 November 2026.

Part of the Data Processing Agreement; see also Security incident response and Subprocessors. Items marked [TO CONFIRM: ...] are not yet established and are not claimed as fact.

No certification is claimed. Codexta holds no SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certificate. This page describes measures that exist; where a fact is not established it is marked, not assumed.

2. Backups

Codexta does not yet publish a backup claim. Until the items below are confirmed, nothing is stated about backups, and deleted data should be assumed to remain in any backup until it expires. Before a claim is made, the following must be true:

  • [TO CONFIRM] The production database is backed up automatically, at least daily.
  • [TO CONFIRM] File storage is covered.
  • [TO CONFIRM] Backups are encrypted at rest and stored off the production host in a separate account or location.
  • [TO CONFIRM] Backup retention is stated and is no longer than what the DPA tells merchants.
  • [TO CONFIRM] A restore has been tested, with date and result.
  • [TO CONFIRM] Access to backups is limited to named people and logged.
  • [TO CONFIRM] Deletion requests are not undone by a restore (erasure handling is re-run after a restore).

3. Test and production separation

In the product:

  • Test-only secrets and local-network switches are loaded only in local development. A host in any other environment that carries them refuses to start.
  • A conversation started in test mode (for example while authoring) runs tools in test mode. Shop tools report that they are not run against a shop, and authored endpoints on a branch use a separate record namespace.
  • Local fake shops and helpdesk apps are used instead of real shops in tests.
  • Custom tenant code is off outside development unless explicitly accepted at startup.

Not evidenced: that production and test run on separate infrastructure, databases and credentials, and that no production customer data is copied into test. [TO CONFIRM]

4. Limiting staff access to customer data

Codexta staff

  • Conversations appear with emails and phone-like numbers hidden. Search runs over the hidden text only.
  • Seeing the unmasked transcript is a separate action that writes an audit row first: no row, no reveal.
  • Working inside a shop requires a written reason, is limited to one shop and one named staff member, expires after 60 minutes and can be revoked. The audit row records who and why.
  • Staff audit rows are append-only, and the merchant can read the rows for its own shop in Settings › Team.
  • Conversations started by a merchant's own team with the back-office assistant are private to their author, and visible only to the shop's owner or admin for their own chats.
  • Staff sign in with a password and a second factor where enabled. [TO CONFIRM: second factor is mandatory for every Codexta staff account in production]

Merchant staff

Roles are Owner, Admin, KB editor, Support manager, Support agent and Viewer; the API refuses what the role may not do. The back-office assistant is not offered to shoppers, and its two changing actions (a customer note or tag, a price) run only after the merchant approves the exact change.

Open items

  • A merchant-side log of who looked up which customer in the back-office assistant is in progress [TO CONFIRM: final name and location]. Until it is live, lookups are visible only in the conversation history of that conversation.
  • Tool results from the back-office assistant (customer name, email, phone, addresses) are sent to the model provider as written, not masked. See Subprocessors.

5. Passwords

Shopify merchants sign in through Shopify; Codexta holds no password for them. Codexta's own accounts (Codexta staff and any non-Shopify login) require a confirmed email. The password policy for those accounts does not yet meet a strong-password requirement. [TO CONFIRM: whether these accounts are used in production for anything other than internal staff, and the intended policy (suggested: minimum 12 characters and a mandatory second factor for staff)]

6. Data loss prevention that exists

  • Credentials are never logged or shown; error text is scrubbed of credential values and token-like strings.
  • Stored report text and the unanswered-question record hide emails and phone-like numbers.
  • Processed webhook bodies and headers are blanked, and the rows are pruned after 30 days.
  • Conversations are deleted nightly by plan retention of 30, 60, 90 or 365 days (see the DPA).
  • Shopify erasure is automated: customer redaction requests, shop redaction requests, and an uninstalled shop is purged after 48 hours.
  • Tenant isolation: every record is filtered by tenant, and PostgreSQL row-level security is available. [TO CONFIRM: that production runs with row-level security effective]
  • Per-IP and per-tenant rate limits on the API and on guest sessions.
  • Downloads expire after 14 days.
  • Webhook and install signatures are verified before use.

Not yet in place: an alert on bulk reads of customer data. [TO CONFIRM]
