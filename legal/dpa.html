2. Subject matter and purposes

Codexta provides an AI chat assistant for shoppers, a back-office assistant for the Merchant's team, and a helpdesk (support console). Codexta processes personal data only to provide those services to the Merchant, namely to:

  • answer shoppers' questions and show them their own orders when they sign in;
  • answer the Merchant's team's questions about its customers, orders and products in the back-office assistant;
  • hand a chat to a person on the Merchant's team and keep tickets, return requests and mail in the helpdesk;
  • report to the Merchant how many chats, unanswered questions and orders followed a chat (counts and order id, total and time only);
  • operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

Codexta does not sell personal data, does not use it for advertising and does not use it for a purpose of its own. Codexta does not use conversation text to train or tune models.

4. Instructions and confidentiality

Codexta processes personal data only on the Merchant's documented instructions, which are this agreement, the Merchant's use of Codexta's settings and the back-office assistant, and its written requests. Codexta will tell the Merchant if it believes an instruction breaks data protection law. Codexta staff access to a shop's conversations is limited and recorded (section 7). Everyone with access is bound by confidentiality.

The Merchant is responsible for its legal basis for processing, for telling its shoppers that an AI assistant is used (the chat window shows the notice "AI assistant. It can make mistakes, so check important information." with a Privacy link and it cannot be switched off), and for mentioning Codexta in its own shop privacy policy.

7. Security measures

These are the measures that exist in the product today. Measures not listed here are not claimed. The fuller statement, with the Shopify requirement evidence table, is on the Security and data protection page.

  • Tenant separation. Every shop's conversations, notes and settings are stored under that shop's tenant id and every query is filtered by it. On PostgreSQL, row-level security is available and is effective when the API runs as the non-owner database role in a non-owner database role. [TO CONFIRM: confirm production runs as that role and which database (PostgreSQL or SQL Server) production uses; SQL Server has no row-level security]
  • Encryption in transit. The public site is served over HTTPS with TLS 1.2 and 1.3 and an HSTS header; plain HTTP requests are redirected.
  • Secrets encrypted at rest. Platform access tokens and the shop's integration keys are stored encrypted (AES-256-CBC with an HMAC-SHA256 integrity check) with a key kept outside the database in database mode. Credentials are never logged or shown to the AI.
  • Webhook and install authentication. Shopify webhooks and installs are accepted only after their HMAC signature verifies. Processed webhook bodies are blanked.
  • Limits on Codexta staff. Staff see conversations with emails and phone-like numbers hidden by default. Showing the unmasked text is a separate action that writes an audit row before the text is returned. Acting inside a shop requires a written reason, lasts 60 minutes and is tied to one shop and one named staff member. The audit log is append-only (the database refuses updates and deletes) and the Merchant can see the entries for its shop in Settings › Team.
  • Roles on the Merchant side. Only owners and admins can change the assistant's settings. The back-office assistant is never offered to shoppers, and a change it proposes (a price, a customer note) is made only after the Merchant confirms that exact change.
  • Production separation. Test-only secrets and fake-shop switches are refused at startup in any environment except local Development. A test-mode conversation does not run tools against a real shop.
  • Backups and disk encryption. [TO CONFIRM: backup schedule, retention, location and encryption of the production database and file storage; encryption at rest of the database and disks. The repository does not document these and they are not claimed here]
  • Incident response. Codexta keeps an incident response procedure, summarised on the Security incident response page. [TO CONFIRM: name of the responsible person and out-of-hours contact]
What is not hidden from the language model. Codexta hides emails and phone-like numbers in staff views and in the record of unanswered questions. It does not remove personal data from what is sent to the language model: chat text, and customer and order details the back-office assistant reads for the Merchant's team, are sent as written.

8. Personal data breach

Codexta will notify the Merchant without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the Merchant's data, [TO CONFIRM: proposed: and in any case within 72 hours; Codecta to confirm the timeframe it can commit to]. The notice will say what happened, which data and shops are affected as far as known, what Codexta is doing and who to contact. Codexta will help the Merchant meet its own duty to notify the supervisory authority and affected people, and will tell Shopify where Shopify's rules for apps with protected customer data require it.

9. Data subject requests

Codexta will help the Merchant answer requests for access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, taking into account the nature of the processing. If a shopper writes to Codexta, Codexta will pass the request to the Merchant unless it is about Codexta's own data.

  • Shopify. The customer erasure request that Shopify sends (customers/redact) is handled automatically: the person's conversations, tickets, preferences, uploads and order attributions in Codexta are deleted. The data request (customers/data_request) is received automatically; Codexta provides the Merchant with an export of that person's data held in Codexta. [TO CONFIRM: the data request automation is being built; confirm it is live and how the export reaches the Merchant]
  • WooCommerce. Deleting a customer in WordPress deletes their conversations in Codexta.
  • Shopper control in the widget. The shopper can delete their own conversations with the Delete my chats option in the chat menu. [TO CONFIRM: wording and availability of the Delete my chats option, still being built]
  • Other requests go to codexta@codecta.com and are answered within 30 days.

11. Return and deletion on termination

When the Merchant uninstalls Codexta, Codexta deletes the shop's data after the 48-hour period above (reinstalling within that period keeps it). Before that, or on request to codexta@codecta.com, Codexta will provide an export of the Merchant's data in a common machine-readable format [TO CONFIRM: export format and timeline for a whole-shop export; the repository has a per-person export in progress, not a documented whole-shop export]. Codexta will confirm deletion on request. Data that law requires Codexta to keep stays, with a notice to the Merchant.

12. Audit

Codexta will give the Merchant the information needed to show compliance with this agreement, including the answers to a reasonable security questionnaire. The Merchant may audit Codexta, or appoint an independent auditor, once a year and after a personal data breach, on 30 days' written notice, in working hours and under confidentiality, at the Merchant's cost. Codexta holds no SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certificate. [TO CONFIRM: confirm; add any certificate or penetration test report Codecta can share]
