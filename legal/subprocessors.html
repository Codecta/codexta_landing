Subprocessors

The companies and services that may process a merchant's personal data for Codexta, what they receive and what is hidden first. Last updated 14 November 2026.

Part of the Data Processing Agreement; see also Security and data protection and Security incident response.

The assistant cannot work without a language model. The whole product is the model. Conversation text, and for the merchant back office the shop data the assistant reads (customer name, email, phone, address, orders), is sent to the language-model provider to produce answers. There is no switch or opt-out for sending text to the model. A shopper who does not want this should not use the chat; the shop's own pages, checkout and support channels remain available. The merchant can switch the assistant off for its shop in its Codexta settings (Chat on your store).
What is hidden before it is sent. Nothing is removed from what reaches the language-model provider. Chat text is sent as the shopper wrote it. For the merchant's back-office assistant, the customer and order details it looks up (names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, orders, as listed in the privacy policy) are sent as returned by the shop and are not masked. Codexta hides emails and phone-like numbers only in what its own staff see and in the stored record of unanswered questions.

Language-model providers

Conversation text that the assistant sends to the model is processed by the configured provider. The provider receives the conversation (the shopper's messages, the assistant's instructions, tool results such as product, order and customer data when a tool is used) to produce a reply. Only the two providers below receive conversation data. Both are engaged under their standard commercial contracts, under which, as Codexta understands them, the data is not used for training.

Changes

Codexta will update this page before adding or replacing a subprocessor that handles a merchant's personal data, and will notify merchants by email to the merchant's account contact 30 days before the change takes effect. A merchant may object to the change within that period. Last review: 14 November 2026.
