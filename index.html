Why not build it ourselves?

Developer toolkits give you building blocks. Codexta is the finished building.

Tools like LangChain and the cloud providers’ agent kits are excellent raw materials. Your engineers still have to design the rules, controls, record-keeping and screens—and maintain them. Codexta arrives with all of that in place. See the full comparison →

01

The rules are written by the people who own the process

With a toolkit, business rules live in code only developers can read. In Codexta, each process is a plain-language procedure, like a staff manual, that your team can read, check and change.

02

The controls come built in

Payment confirmations, required disclosures, manager sign-offs, hand-over to a colleague and a tamper-evident record come with the platform. With a toolkit, your team designs and builds each of them.

03

Codexta gives the AI only the one procedure it needs for each request, so it stays focused. That is why it works on smaller open models running on your own hardware, not only on the largest paid ones.

What’s built in

The controls your risk and compliance teams will ask about.

These come with the platform. You don’t design them from scratch, and you can test every one of them in a pilot.

01 / Focus

Each job sees only what it needs

A job gets only the information and systems it needs. When one job hands over to another, only the facts required are passed on, not the whole conversation.

Check in the pilot: see exactly what each job could see.
02 / Policy

Your procedures, with steps locked in

Your operating procedures become written, versioned instructions. Where steps, their order or approvals must not be skipped, the system enforces them.

Check in the pilot: write up one real procedure and try to break it.
03 / Access

Everyone sees only their part

What each person can see and do follows their role and the procedure in use. Only authorised staff can change how the system behaves.

Check in the pilot: run the same process as two different roles.
04 / Change

Test changes before they go live

New procedures, forms and screens are saved as drafts and tested first. Customers only see a change when someone switches it on.

Check in the pilot: change a draft and confirm customers still see the old version.
05 / Passwords

Passwords the AI never sees

Passwords typed into a conversation are encrypted the moment they’re entered and never shown to the AI. System passwords live in a separate encrypted store, never in procedures.

Check in the pilot: review how keys and system passwords are stored.
06 / Evidence

A record you can verify

Every conversation can be exported with proof that it hasn’t been altered, so reviewers can check it independently instead of taking our word for it.

Check in the pilot: export a conversation and verify it yourself.
Your team changes it

Change a process without waiting for a developer.

Authorised people can add or change procedures, forms, screens and connections to your systems while Codexta is running. They describe the change in a conversation; it is checked and tested before it is saved, and goes live only when someone decides.

01 / Author

Describe it, don’t deploy it

New procedures, forms, result screens and connections to your systems can be described in plain language and added while the system runs. No rebuild, no restart, no waiting for a release.

Check in the pilot: add one new connection and use it the same day.
02 / Version

Draft, test, then activate

Every change is checked and refused if it doesn’t work. Each save becomes a version you can test and compare, and customers only see it when someone switches it on. You can always switch back.

Check in the pilot: save a second version, test it, and confirm customers still get the first.
03 / Restrict

Only the right people can change it

Changing how Codexta behaves is itself a permission. Everyday users never get it; only the people responsible for the process do.

Check in the pilot: open the same workspace as an editor and as a regular user.

Direct answers

What buyers ask first.

Is this like LangChain, or the agent kits from Google and Microsoft?

Those are toolkits for engineers to build AI applications from scratch. Codexta is a finished platform: the procedures, locked steps, approvals, record-keeping and screens are already in place, and your business team can change the procedures without a developer. Your engineers could build much of it with a toolkit. The question is whether you want to fund building and maintaining it.

Does it replace our current software?

No. Codexta works through the systems you already have. Your systems of record stay exactly where they are.

Can the AI act without a person?

Only where you allow it. Looking things up can happen straight away, while payments, refunds, decisions and other sensitive actions can be made to wait for the right person. We agree those boundaries with you in the pilot.

Which AI does it use, and where does our data go?

Your choice: smaller open models on your own hardware, so conversations never leave your network, or your existing provider on your own contract. We never resell AI usage. See the two options →

How is it priced?

It depends on how you run it. In your own organisation: no licence fee and no per-user charges, just a one-time setup fee, integration work scoped per project, and a yearly support fee. As an app on a platform such as an online store: a monthly plan billed through that platform’s marketplace. See what’s included →

Can someone trick the AI into breaking the rules?

No serious supplier should claim that risk disappears. What we can say is that the most important rules are enforced by the system itself, not by the AI, so talking the AI round doesn’t get past them. Your security review should still test this in your environment.

Where are your certifications and benchmark results?

We don’t show badges or headline numbers we can’t back up. Ask us for the evidence that matters to your deployment, and we’ll agree the measures for your pilot up front.
