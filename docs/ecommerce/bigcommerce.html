Install Codexta on BigCommerce

Install the app from your control panel. The chat button is added to your store for you.

  1. In your BigCommerce control panel, open Apps and install Codexta.

  2. Approve the access BigCommerce asks for. You see a page saying Codexta is installed.

  3. Open Codexta from Apps. It opens inside your control panel.

  4. Choose a plan. Until you do, the assistant does not answer.

  5. Press Set up my assistant on Overview and answer the questions.

  6. Open your storefront and look for the chat button.

Tip. Codexta adds its chat script to your storefront itself. You do not edit your theme. Only the store owner can uninstall the app.
