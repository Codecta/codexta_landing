Find or block a shopper

Everyone who chatted, with their conversations in one click.

Open it
  • Shopify Shopify admin › Apps › Codexta
  • WooCommerce WooCommerce › Codexta Assistant
  • Other systems The admin link in your Codexta welcome email

  1. Open Shoppers in the top menu.

  2. Type a name or email in Search shoppers.

  3. Press Conversations on a row to read their chats.

  4. To stop someone chatting, press Block. Tick Blocked only to list blocked shoppers.

Tip. A blocked shopper is told they reached today’s limit, never that they are blocked. Only the Owner and Admins can block.
