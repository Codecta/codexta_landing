Online shops

Install Codexta on your shop platform, then set it up like any other Codexta account.

  • ShopifyInstall from the Shopify App Store.
  • WooCommerceUpload the plugin and connect your shop.
  • ShopwareInstall the Codexta app in your Administration.
  • BigCommerceInstall the Codexta app from your control panel.

Then, for shops

  • Chat on your storefrontSee, move or hide the chat button.
  • Orders and productsAsk the back-office assistant about orders and products.
  • ShoppersFind or block someone who chatted.
