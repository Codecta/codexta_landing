Words used in these docs

Assistant
The chat that answers your customers on your site or application. In your admin there is a second one, the Store assistant, for you and your team.
Knowledge base and notes
What the assistant knows: your own written answers (shipping, returns, sizing…), one topic per note. The assistant answers customers from these, and does not know what is not written there.
Guideline
A set of written rules for one kind of work, such as helping customers find products. Codexta provides them; you can switch them on or off.
Branch
A draft copy of your assistant (guidelines, knowledge-base articles, tools and cards). Chats on a branch use the drafts; your shoppers keep the live version until you publish it. See the Authoring guide.
Test mode
A box in Assistant › Chat: the assistant goes through the same steps, but places no real orders, sends no emails and changes nothing in your shop.
Tool and card
A tool is something the assistant can do in your shop (search products, add to cart, read an order); a card is how a result looks in the chat. A tool is offered only with the guidelines it names.
Codexta review
Before a tool or card your store changed runs anywhere, Codexta reads and approves that exact code. Guideline and article changes need no review.
Lesson
A short nudge on how the assistant behaves, such as “ask for the budget before suggesting a gift”. You add it in the chat or on the Knowledge base page’s Lessons tab. It applies from the next chat.
Preference
How one person likes to be helped, such as “short answers”. Each customer or colleague sets their own.
Guest
A customer who chats without signing in to your site or application. Guests can shop with the assistant; their own orders and returns need signing in.
Plan feature
Something only some plans include, such as authoring or Instagram. Settings › Plan shows which ones you have.
Authoring
Changing the assistant’s guidelines, or adding your own, by describing the change in a chat.
Publish
Making a tried change live for your customers. Until then it is a draft only you can test.
Roles
What each colleague may see and do: Owner, Admin, KB editor (KB = knowledge base), Support manager, Support agent, Viewer.
