Codexta for Shopify

Install the app, choose a plan and switch it on. Then teach the assistant about your shop.

Prefer to watch? The video tutorial shows the same steps.

Install

  1. Install Codexta from the Shopify App Store and approve the access Shopify asks for.

  2. Choose a plan on Shopify’s plan page and approve it. Until you do, the assistant does not answer.

  3. In Codexta, open Overview and press Open the theme editor under Set up your assistant.

  4. Under App embeds, switch on Codexta assistant, then press Save.

  5. Back on Overview, press Set up my assistant and answer the questions. Skip anything you like.

  6. Open your shop and look for the chat button in the corner.

Tip. You are the Owner in Codexta when your Shopify account owner installs it. Colleagues get access under Team and roles.

Then teach your assistant

The same in every shop. Start with the first two.

  • Name, greeting and questionsWhat shoppers see when the chat opens.
  • Add an articleWhat your assistant knows: delivery, returns, sizes.
  • Import a web page or PDFTurn pages and files you already have into articles.
  • Add a lessonHow your assistant behaves: a short nudge.
  • LanguagesThe chat’s language and your shoppers’.
  • Chat on your storefrontSee, move or hide the chat button.

Choose which discounts the assistant can offer

The assistant only offers a discount you have marked for it. Unmarked discounts stay private, so internal codes are safe.

  1. In Shopify, open Discounts and open the discount.

  2. Add #aiassistant to its Title, for example Summer 10% #aiassistant, and save. Codes and automatic discounts both work.

  3. Ask the assistant “Is there a discount?” to check. Shoppers see the title without the mark.

Tip. Only active discounts are offered, and only the first 25 active ones are read. To stop offering one, remove the mark.

Choose the pages that show the chat

The chat button is on every page by default. To keep it off some pages, open Settings › General › Pages with the chat, type one pattern per line and press Save settings. Leave it empty to show the chat everywhere.

  • ^/$The home page only.
  • ^/products/Every product page.
  • ^/(?!cart|checkout)Everywhere except the cart and checkout.

Tip. Put every exclusion in one line, as above: two separate lines would each let the other page through. Write paths as they appear in your address bar. More examples are in the full reference.

Uninstall and your data

Uninstalling cancels your Shopify subscription and removes Codexta’s access to your shop. All of your shop’s data is deleted 48 hours later. Reinstall before then and your setup comes back; you choose a plan again.

While installed, conversations older than your plan’s retention are deleted every night. When a customer asks Shopify for their data, the export is under Settings › Data requests for 30 days. Payments never pass through Codexta. See the privacy policy.
