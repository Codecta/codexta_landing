The support console: your helpdesk

The support console is where your team answers customers in person. Live chats the assistant hands over, tickets, return requests and emails all arrive here as cases. You reply, add notes for colleagues, pass cases on and close them when they are solved. It works the same in every shop Codexta connects to.

Places are written as Codexta › Support console › Inbox. In Shopify, Codexta is under Shopify admin › Apps › Codexta; in WooCommerce, under WooCommerce › Codexta Assistant. Things you can type look like “I want to talk to a real person”. Back to the Shopify guide or the WooCommerce guide. Setting things up (team, queues, hours, channels, forms, automation)? Use the setup guide. New words are explained in the glossary.

01

What the console is

Every request that needs a person becomes a case with its own number, starting at 10001. A case is either a chat (a live conversation in the chat window on your shop) or a ticket (a request your team answers later, usually by email).

How do I open the console?

  • Who: Owner, Admin, Support manager, Support agent
  • Plan: any
  • Shopify: Shopify admin › Apps › Codexta › Support console in the sidebar.
  • WooCommerce: WooCommerce › Codexta Assistant, then Support console in Codexta’s menu bar.
  • From the Overview: the Support card has Open the support console.

The console has these sections on the left: Inbox, My dashboard, Contacts, Help center and Calls. Support managers, Admins and the Owner also see Team and Reports. Only the Owner and Admins see Settings. The New ticket button and the other lists are above the case list.

In WooCommerce the console is a window of fixed height (720 px) inside the WordPress page. Its lists and the open conversation scroll inside that window; the rest of the WordPress page scrolls as usual.

What do the case states mean?

StateMeaning
NewJust arrived. Nobody has worked on it yet.
OpenSomeone is working on it.
PendingWaiting, for example for the customer, until a time you choose. Response targets pause meanwhile.
ResolvedSolved. It opens again if the customer writes back.
ClosedFinished for good.

What is already set up for a new shop?

You can start answering right away. A new shop gets one queue called Support (the default), a Live chat channel, two support levels (Support and Tier 2), the priorities Low, Normal, High and Urgent, and Business hours of Monday to Friday, 08:00–17:00 UTC. Change any of them under Settings.

02

How a day in the helpdesk goes

One example from the shopper’s question to the rating. Each step links to the part that explains it.

Example: a shopper asks something the assistant cannot answer

  • Who: shopper, then Owner, Admin, Support manager, Support agent
  • Plan: any (tickets switched on)
  1. The shopper asks. In your shop’s chat they type “Do you ship to Montenegro?” Nothing in your notes answers it.
  2. The assistant offers support. It shows one card:
    SHOPPER SEESI couldn’t find an answer to that. Shall I pass your question to support?
    The card has a Send to support button and, if you set them, Chat on WhatsApp, Chat on Viber and Call us (see contact options). If the assistant cannot answer at all (the plan’s limit is reached, or it is not available), the shopper sees a short contact form instead.
  3. A ticket or a live chat starts. After Send to support the assistant opens a ticket and gives the shopper its number (it asks only for the contact details it still needs). A shopper who asks for a person gets a live chat if your team is available. Either way it arrives as a case, and the customer gets an email that the request arrived.
  4. You pick it up. The case is in Unassigned, or already with an online colleague if its queue assigns automatically. Press Assign to me. Open the full transcript shows what the assistant said. See the Inbox.
  5. You reply. Write in Reply, add an Internal note for colleagues if needed, and send. A ticket reply reaches the customer by email; a live chat reply appears in their chat window. See working on a case.
  6. You resolve it. Press Resolve and, if you like, type what solved it. If the customer writes again, the case opens again.
  7. The customer rates it. If the queue has Ask for a satisfaction rating (CSAT) on, the customer gets one survey and rates 1 to 5. The answer appears in Reports › CSAT.
  8. You learn from it. Suggested FAQs in the help center turn recurring answers into articles, and reports show what the assistant resolved and what it handed over.

Setting this up for the first time? Follow the order in the setup guide.

04

Switch the helpdesk on

How do I switch live chat, tickets or email intake on?

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: any

Codexta › Support console › Settings › Backends

  1. Switch on what your shop offers. Each switch takes a few seconds:
    • Tickets and incident reports: Customers report a problem and follow their own tickets in the shop chat.
    • Live chat with a person: Customers ask the shop chat for a human and keep typing to your agent.
    • Email intake: Mail to your email channels becomes tickets. It needs an email channel first (see Email).

The Overview’s Support card shows which parts are on and how many cases are open.

How do I set when live chat is open?

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: any

Codexta › Support console › Settings › Live chat

  1. Under Live chat is, choose Always open, Follow business hours or Off.
  2. With Follow business hours, pick which hours. The times, days and holidays themselves are in the Business hours tab (see business hours).

A chat that is already open is never cut off. Outside these times, the assistant tells the shopper when people are available and opens a ticket for them instead.

How do I pause live chat for a while?

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: any

Codexta › Overview › Support (or Support console › Settings › Live chat)

  1. On the Overview, press Pause for 1 hour or Pause until tomorrow. The Live chat tab also offers 30 minutes and a time of your choice under Pause live chat for a while.
  2. The pause ends by itself. To end it early, press Resume now.
05

The Inbox

The Inbox lists cases on the left and shows the open case on the right. A panel of Details sits beside it.

Which lists are there?

  • Who: everyone with the console
  • Plan: any
  • Mine: cases you own (new, open, pending and resolved). Unassigned: nobody owns them yet. Unattended: still waiting for a first reply.
  • Mentions: colleagues mentioned you in a note. Participating: cases you watch or take part in.
  • All open, Pending, Resolved and Closed: cases by state.
  • Switch between All, Tickets and Chats at the top.
  • Under My views are the filters you saved. Under Labels are the labels set to show in the sidebar. A number beside a list counts its unread cases.
  • A small indicator shows the connection: Live, Connecting…, Reconnecting… or Offline · refreshing every 30s.

How do I find a case, filter and sort?

  1. Type in the search box (or press /) to search by words, number or customer. The box remembers Recent searches (Clear removes them).
  2. Press Filters to narrow the list. You can filter by Queue, Priority, Tier, Channel, Owner (Me, Unassigned or an email), Tag, Attribute (key:value) for your own fields, Customer email and Overdue only. The button shows how many filters are on; clear removes them.
  3. For more exact questions use Cases matching all conditions or any condition, then Add condition: choose what (Status, Priority, Queue, Tier, Channel, Kind, Source, Assignee, Label, Title, Customer email, Customer name, Created, Last activity, Resolved, or one of your own attributes), how (is, is not, contains, does not contain, starts with, is set, is not set, and for dates is after, is before, is older than (days)) and the value.
  4. Sort changes the order: Newest first, Oldest first, Last activity, Priority: urgent first, Resolution due soonest or Waiting longest for a reply. The list button switches between a full and a compact list.
  5. To keep a filter, press Save as view (the bookmark) and give it a name. It appears under My views; the bin beside it deletes the view.

Example. To see every case that has waited more than a day: Filters › Add condition › Last activity is older than (days) 1, sort by Waiting longest for a reply, then Save as view as Stale.

How do I tell the team I am available?

Set My availability to Online, Busy or Offline. The console tells Codexta every minute that you are still there. When a queue assigns cases automatically, new cases go only to people who are online with the console open.

How do I get notified, and what else is in My settings?

Codexta › Support console › the bell › My settings

  • The bell shows new notifications (Notifications ({n} unread)); Mark all read clears them. You are notified when A case is assigned to me, A customer writes on my case, Someone mentions me, A case misses its first-response target or A case misses its resolution target, or A new case arrives in a queue I review.
  • Under In this browser choose Play a sound and Pop-up when the console is in the background.
  • Under Push to this device, press Notify me here even when the console is closed. Send a test sends a test message to your devices.
  • Under Also email me when, tick the events above you want by email too.
  • My reply signature is added to your replies (Signature in the composer). Console language can follow your browser. Text size goes from Smaller to Largest. Translate customer messages into my language translates them as you read.
  • My API tokens makes personal tokens for a developer; see API tokens.

In a note you can also mention a team: type @ and pick the team (everyone in that queue).

06

Live chat: take over from the assistant

When a shopper explicitly asks for a person, the assistant hands the chat to your team. The shopper keeps typing in the same chat window.

Example: a shopper asks for a person

  • Who: Owner, Admin, Support manager, Support agent
  • Plan: any (live chat switched on)
  1. The shopper types “I want to talk to a real person”. A guest (not signed in) is first asked for a name and an email address.
  2. The chat window shows:
    SHOPPER SEESLooking for an available agent…
  3. In the console, the chat appears in Unassigned (and on the Team page under chats waiting). Open it. You see the whole conversation with the assistant so far.
  4. Press Assign to me. Write in Reply and send with Enter. The shopper now sees:
    SHOPPER SEESConnected to a human agent
  5. Use Internal note for anything only your team should read.
  6. When the question is answered, press Resolve. You can type what solved it.

The shopper can leave with End chat. They are asked End this conversation? and then return to the assistant.

Guests cannot send files in a live chat. Under Settings › Channels, the live chat channel sets what the shopper sees above your replies. With Friendly (the agent's name) they see the agent’s Display name (optional) from Settings › Members, or else the first part of their email address. With Professional (no personal name) they see only “Support agent”.

What happens when nobody is available?

Outside your live chat hours, while paused, or when live chat is off, nobody is connected. The assistant says when people are available and, in the same answer, opens a ticket with the shopper’s problem. It asks only for the contact details it still needs and gives the shopper the ticket number.

07

Contact options: WhatsApp, Viber and phone

Contact options are buttons that let a shopper reach you directly when the assistant cannot help. They are shop settings, not a support console tab, and they work without any connected channel: each button simply opens the shopper’s own app.

How do I add the WhatsApp, Viber and Call buttons?

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: any

Shopify admin › Apps › Codexta › Settings › General › Contact options

WooCommerce › Codexta Assistant › Settings › General › Contact options

  1. Find the panel Contact options. It says Optional. and asks for the international format.
  2. Type your numbers: WhatsApp number, Viber number and Phone number (calls), for example +387 61 123 456.
  3. Leave a field empty to hide its button.
  4. Press Save settings.

What a number may look like. It must be an international number of 7 to 15 digits, starting with the country code. Spaces, dashes, dots and brackets are dropped, and a leading 00 becomes +. A number that starts with 0 after the country code is refused, so write +387 61 123 456, not 061 123 456. A wrong number is refused with must be an international phone number of 7 to 15 digits, like +387 61 123 456. Codexta stores it as +38761123456.

Where do shoppers see the buttons?

WhereWhenWhat the shopper sees
The “pass it to support?” cardThe assistant could not answer (nothing on file, or it cannot tell).I couldn’t find an answer to that. Shall I pass your question to support? with Send to support, then Chat on WhatsApp, Chat on Viber and Call us for each number you set. Send to support opens a ticket (tickets).
The contact formThe assistant cannot answer at all, for example the plan’s limit is reached or it is not available. The form replaces the message box.A line Or contact us directly: with the same buttons, then Your name, Your email, Subject (optional), Your message and Send. After sending: Thank you, your message has been sent. We’ll reply by email. If the problem may pass, Try the assistant again brings the message box back.

A message sent with the form opens a ticket in your helpdesk (category widget-contact), up to 5,000 characters, subject to the same guest limits as other guest tickets. A shopper who types a wrong email, or sends too many, reads the reason in the form.

What exactly do the buttons do, and what are the limits?

  • Chat on WhatsApp opens wa.me/your-number in a new tab. On the card it comes with the shopper’s question already typed in (one short line, at most 500 characters), so the shopper only presses send. On the contact form it opens an empty chat.
  • Chat on Viber opens the Viber app on the shopper’s device (viber://chat). On the card it passes the same question as a draft through a Viber parameter that Viber does not document, so the text may not appear. The chat opens either way.
  • Call us opens the phone’s dialler (tel:) in the same window. On a computer, it needs an app that can place calls.
  • Only numbers you set show a button. The assistant never supplies or mentions a number itself.
  • The buttons only start a conversation in WhatsApp, Viber or the phone app. Answers happen there, in your own app or on your own phone, not in the support console. Codexta does not check that a number is registered with WhatsApp or Viber.
  • Button names appear in the chat window’s languages: English, Croatian, Bosnian, German, French and Spanish.

Want WhatsApp conversations to arrive in Codexta itself? That is a connected messaging channel, set up on the Integrations (“Connect”) page; see other channels. It is separate from these buttons.

08

Tickets and return requests

Where do tickets come from?

  • Plan: any
  • A problem reported in the chat. With tickets switched on, a shopper who says something is not working gets help from your knowledge base first. Only when that does not solve it does the assistant open a ticket.
  • A return from a guest or an Instagram message. The shopper gives a name, an email and photos; a photo is required.
  • A return from a signed-in WooCommerce customer. The assistant checks the order and the photos and opens a return ticket, tagged return. On Shopify, a signed-in customer’s return is requested on the order in Shopify instead, and you decide it there.
  • Email to your support address, when email intake is on.
  • Your own team, with the New ticket button (see below).
  • A form on your store, built under Settings › Forms (how). Each submission is a ticket labelled with the form’s key.
  • The contact form in the chat window, when the assistant cannot answer (see contact options).
  • A phone call or voicemail, logged on the caller’s case (see calls).

The customer gets an email saying their request arrived, and your replies reach them by email. Signed-in shoppers can also ask about their own tickets in the shop chat.

How do I open a ticket myself?

  • Who: Owner, Admin, Support manager, Support agent
  • Plan: any
  1. Press New ticket at the top of the lists (or use the command bar, Ctrl/⌘ K).
  2. Fill in Title and Description. Both are required.
  3. Optionally set Priority and Queue (otherwise Default), Customer email (optional) and Tags (comma separated).
  4. Press Create ticket.

Add the customer’s email so your replies reach them. This is handy for a phone call or a visit in your shop.

Example: handle a return request

  • Who: Owner, Admin, Support manager, Support agent
  • Plan: any
  1. Open the ticket from Unassigned. Its description lists the order, the item, the delivery date, whether it is in time, and the photo checks the assistant made.
  2. Look at the photos under Files in the details.
  3. Press Assign to me, then Reply, for example: “Thanks, your return is approved. Please send the item to …” The customer gets it by email.
  4. Process the refund in your shop’s admin as usual. The console does not move money.
  5. Press Resolve.

In the back-office chat you can also ask “Any open return requests” for a quick list. Replying and closing happen here in the console.

09

Working on a case

How do I reply or add a note?

  1. Choose Reply (the customer sees it) or Internal note (only your team sees it).
  2. In a note, type @ to mention a colleague. They get a notification.
  3. Type / to insert a saved reply. Attach files with Attach or drop them in, or record a Voice note.
  4. For an email ticket, Cc / Bcc / subject adds recipients. The toolbar has Bold, Italic, Link, Bulleted list and Emoji, and Run a macro and Signature.
  5. Press Send reply or Add note. In a live chat Enter sends and Shift+Enter makes a new line. In a ticket Enter makes a new line and Ctrl/Cmd+Enter sends.

AI assist helps with the text: Suggest a reply, Summarize the case, Improve writing, Fix spelling and grammar, Shorten, Simplify, a tone (Tone: friendly, professional, casual, confident or straightforward), Make formal, Expand, Change it as I say… or a translation into the customer’s language. A suggested summary can be added as an internal note. Undo AI puts your own text back.

Example: pass a case to a colleague

  1. Open the case and its Details.
  2. Under Owner, search the colleague by name or email and pick them.
  3. Add an Internal note such as “@Ana can you check the delivery? The parcel shows delivered.”

Only colleagues allowed to work at the case’s level are offered. To move it to specialists instead, press Escalate and answer Why escalate to the next tier?. To move it to another team, use Move to queue…. De-escalate moves it back down a tier.

How do I put a case on hold, resolve or close it?

  • Pend: wait until 1 hour, Tomorrow, Next week, Next month or a date you choose. It comes back by itself.
  • Resolve: the problem is solved. You can write Resolution (optional):. If the customer writes again, the case opens again. A case cannot be resolved while a required field is empty (see fields).
  • Close: finished for good, with an optional reason. Reopen brings a case back.

What is in the Details panel?

The State, Owner, Queue / Tier, Priority, tags, the due times of the response targets, Participants (press Watch to follow a case), your own Attributes, Files, the customer’s Other cases of this customer, and where the chat started (Came from, page, browser). A chat started with the assistant has Open the full transcript. When your shop platform can list orders, Orders shows the customer’s orders: number (a link to the shop admin), total, date, payment and delivery status and items. Suggest labels lets the AI propose tags. The Activity list at the bottom shows every change and who made it. Apps you add (dashboard apps) are tabs beside Details.

What can I do with one message?

Each message has actions: Reply to this message, Forward by email, Translate, Copy text, Save as a saved reply, Make internal or Make visible to the customer, and Copy link to this message and Delete message (Admins can delete any message; an agent can delete their own). On an assistant’s answer, Report marks it as wrong or unsuitable, so your team can fix the knowledge base.

What else is in the case header?

  • Search this case (the magnifier) finds words in the conversation; Enter jumps to the next match, Shift+Enter to the earlier one.
  • Mark unread goes back to the list and keeps the case marked as unread.
  • Email the conversation sends a transcript to an address you type (Send the transcript to:).
  • Macro… runs a macro (macros).
  • Call the customer appears on a case that began as a phone call. The first time it asks for your own phone number: Twilio rings you first, then connects you to the customer.
  • Delete case at the end of the details is only for Admins and asks for confirmation. It is written to the audit log.

How do I change many cases at once?

Tick cases in the list (or Select all shown). The bar shows {n} selected and offers Assign to me, Assign… (you type the colleague’s email), Unassign, Tag…, Untag…, State…, Priority… and Macro…. The cross clears the selection.

10

Your team: members, queues and tiers

A queue is a group of cases a team works, for example Support or Billing. A tier is a support level: first line, then specialists. Each person has a place in a queue at a tier.

Example: add a colleague to the helpdesk

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: any
  1. Give them a role in Codexta’s Settings › Team: Support agent or Support manager.
  2. Ask them to open the support console once. They are then a member of the Support queue at the first tier, and can take cases there.
  3. To put them in another queue or at a higher tier, open Codexta › Support console › Settings › Members and press Add member. Pick them under Team member, choose a Queue (or All queues) and a Tier, and save. They can take cases at that tier or lower.
  4. Tick Reviews new requests if they should get an email for every new ticket in that queue.

The Owner and Admins are added the same way, at the highest tier, so a case escalated to them can always be taken. To stop someone getting cases, open their row here and press Deactivate. Codexta never reactivates a row you deactivated; you can bring it back yourself with Reactivate. Every field of a member, with an example: the setup guide.

How do I add a queue and spread work fairly?

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: any

Codexta › Support console › Settings › Queues

  • Add queue with a code, name, icon and colour. Exactly one queue is the Default queue; cases with no queue land there.
  • Assign new cases automatically gives each new case to an online member, by Balanced (fewest open cases) or Round robin (whoever waited longest). You can cap the open cases per person with At most open cases per member and limit how fast cases are handed to one person with Fair distribution. Automatic assignment goes to a member at the case’s tier or above.
  • Auto-resolve after resolves cases with no activity for that long. Never auto-resolve a case waiting on an agent keeps cases where the customer wrote last.
  • Ask for a satisfaction rating (CSAT): see ratings.
  • Every field, with an example: the setup guide.

How do tiers and escalation work?

Codexta › Support console › Settings › Tiers

Each tier has a rank; higher is more senior. A member can own cases at their tier or lower. Escalate moves a case up to the next tier. De-escalate moves it back down. A queue can also be set to Auto-escalate on SLA breach: a case that misses its target moves up a tier and loses its owner. Add tiers under Settings › Tiers (steps).

How do I limit what an agent sees?

Codexta › Support console › Settings › Roles

  1. Press Add role and name it.
  2. Under Cases, choose Every case of their queues, Unassigned cases and their own or Only their own and those they take part in.
  3. Under Also, allow Manage contacts, Manage the help center or See reports.
  4. Give the role to a member under Settings › Members (Member role).

Admins are never limited by a custom role. More: custom roles in the setup guide.

11

Priorities, response targets and hours

A response target (SLA) is how fast your team promises to answer. Each case shows when its first reply, its next reply and its resolution are due. A missed target shows as SLA breached.

How do I set the targets?

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: any
  • By priority (Settings › Priorities; steps): each priority has a First response target and a Resolution target. New cases get the default priority.
  • By rule (Settings › SLA policies; steps): a policy sets targets for cases that match its conditions, such as one channel, label or queue. The first matching policy wins; with none, the priority’s targets apply.

How do I set business hours and holidays?

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: any

Codexta › Support console › Settings › Business hours

  1. Open Business hours (or Add business hours).
  2. Set the Time zone first, then the Weekly hours per day. A day can have several windows.
  3. Add Holidays. On a holiday the targets do not count. Each day is ticked open with a start and end time; Add window adds a second one. Every field: the setup guide.
  4. Choose these hours on a queue so its targets count only while open, and in the Live chat tab to open live chat only then.
12

Saved replies, labels and fields

Example: create a saved reply

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: any

Codexta › Support console › Settings › Saved replies

  1. Press Add.
  2. Short code: refund. Text: Hi {{customer.name}}, your refund for request #{{ticket.number}} is on its way. It shows on your account within 5 working days. {{agent.name}}
  3. Press Save.
  4. In a reply, type /refund and pick it. The names and the number are filled in.

The fields you can use are {{customer.name}}, {{customer.email}}, {{agent.name}}, {{agent.email}}, {{ticket.number}} and {{ticket.title}}. Any agent can also save a good answer from a message with Save as a saved reply. If a name cannot be filled, the composer warns before sending.

How do I use labels?

Codexta › Support console › Settings › Labels

A label is a tag with a colour and a meaning, such as billing. Tick Show in the sidebar to get a list of every case with that label. Renaming a label renames it on every case. In a case, Suggest labels lets the AI propose fitting ones.

How do I add my own fields?

Codexta › Support console › Settings › Attributes

Add fields to cases or contacts, such as an order number. Agents fill them in the case details. Tick Required before resolving to make sure nobody resolves a case without it. The types are text, number, currency, percent, link, date, list and checkbox; steps in the setup guide.

13

Macros and automation

How do I do several steps in one click?

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: any

Codexta › Support console › Settings › Macros

A macro is a list of actions you run on a case in one click, for example Add tags billing, Set priority High and Move to queue Billing. Make it for Only me or Everyone. Run it from the case with Macro…, or on many cases with the bulk bar.

How do I make things happen by themselves?

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: any

Codexta › Support console › Settings › Automation

A rule runs actions on a case when something happens and its conditions match. For example: When A case is created, If Title / subject contains “invoice”, Then Move to queue Billing. Other triggers include The customer has not answered for… and Nobody from the team has answered for…. Rules are checked every few minutes and run once per case. A rule’s own changes never start another rule. Full list of triggers, conditions and actions: the setup guide.

14

Email

How do I send email from my own address?

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: any

Codexta › Support console › Settings › Email

  1. Enter your SMTP server, Port, Username, Password and the From address customers see and reply to. Your mail provider gives you these.
  2. Press Save, then type an address in Send a test to (empty: your own email) and press Test.
  3. Optionally tick Use the branded layout: your logo and name on a band of your colour, and your footer, on every email.

Without your own server, Codexta’s platform mail sends for you.

How do I turn incoming email into tickets?

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: any
  1. Ask us to connect your support mailbox. For security, the mailbox password is kept by Codexta, never typed into the console.
  2. In Settings › Channels, press Add channel, choose the kind Email, enter the address and choose the mailbox we set up.
  3. Switch Email intake on in Settings › Backends.

Instead of a mailbox we read, you can forward your support address to the channel’s Forwarding address, when one is shown in the channel. Replies still leave from the mailbox. The channel fields are in the setup guide.

If some mail could not be filed, the Overview’s Support card says so. Open that mail in your mailbox.

15

Contacts

What can I do with contacts?

  • Who: everyone with the console (changes may need Manage contacts)
  • Plan: any

Codexta › Support console › Contacts

Contacts has two views at the top: People and Companies. A contact is made for everyone who chats, writes or is created by your team.

  • Search by Name, email or phone. Filter by Company, Label, Online now and Contact type, and sort by Last activity, Name, Email, Newest first or Oldest first.
  • Save as segment keeps the filter under a name you give it (Name this segment).
  • Import CSV adds people (you are told how many were new, updated and skipped). Export CSV downloads the list.
  • Tick several people to Add label, Remove label, Block, Unblock or Delete them together. Deleting erases every case of theirs and cannot be undone.

How do I look at and change one contact?

  • Who: everyone with the console (changes need Manage contacts)
  1. Click a person. The panel shows Contact name, Contact email, Phone, Contact company, Labels (comma separated), the Contact type and profile links such as Website and social networks. Since says when the contact began.
  2. The type is worked out for you (a customer has a shop account, a lead has an email or phone, a visitor has neither). Pick a type yourself to override it.
  3. Change what you need and press Save.
  4. Below are the contact’s Cases, Files and Notes. Write a note for the team in New contact note and press Add.
  • Merge into…: type the email of the other contact. You are asked to confirm; their cases and notes move over and the first record is removed.
  • Block / Unblock: a blocked contact cannot open new requests, chat or write in; their emails are not filed.
  • Delete and erase: deletes the contact and every case of theirs. This cannot be undone.

How do companies work?

  • Who: everyone with the console (changes need Manage contacts)
  1. Open Companies at the top of Contacts and press Add.
  2. Fill in Company name, Company domain (new contacts whose email is at this domain join the company by themselves) and Company description. Press Save.
  3. Open a company to see its People and Cases. Put a person in a company with Contact company on their panel.

Deleting a company asks Delete the company {name}? Its people stay, without a company.

These apps are not on every plan. On Shopify, see Shopify’s plan page; on WooCommerce, ask us. Without one you see Your plan does not include this app. Upgrade the plan to use it. Keys are checked when you save and never shown again.

Example: connect Slack

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: not on every plan

Codexta › Support console › Settings › Apps

  1. In Slack, create an app for your workspace and invite it to the channel your team uses. Copy its bot token, its signing secret and the channel id (it starts with C).
  2. In the console, fill in Slack bot token, Slack signing secret and Slack channel id.
  3. Choose the Slack mode:
    • Two-way: replies in Slack go to the customer. Start a reply with note: to keep it internal.
    • Alert only: answer in the console.
  4. Press Connect. Copy the Event Request URL shown into your Slack app’s event settings.

New cases are then posted to the channel, and the conversation follows in the thread.

What do the other apps do?

  • Linear: create a Linear issue from a case, or link one, with Create issue in the details. The link is noted on the case.
  • Stripe: with a restricted key that reads customers, subscriptions and invoices, the case details show the customer’s Stripe information.
  • Notion: pages shared with your Notion integration can be imported into your knowledge base (Knowledge base › Add document › or from Notion:) and kept in sync.

Dashboards, reports and ratings

What is on My dashboard?

  • Who: everyone with the console

Your cases right now and today. The tiles are Open, Waiting on me, Overdue, Breached and Resolved today, then My first response vs team, My resolution time vs team and Reopen rate vs team. Urgent lists your open cases, soonest due first (click one to open it); Open by priority counts them. Today shows what was Newly assigned to you and what you Escalated since midnight.

How do I see the whole team right now?

  • Who: Owner, Admin, Support manager

Codexta › Support console › Team

A live page for the whole helpdesk: Breached open and Chats waiting (unassigned live chats, longest wait first; click one to pick it up), a table of Queues with Open, Unassigned, Overdue and Oldest open for each queue and tier, and Agent load with each agent’s Open, Pending, Overdue and Last activity. Hide agents with no cases keeps it short.

What do the reports show and how do I use them?

  • Who: Owner, Admin, Support manager (or a custom role with See reports)

Codexta › Support console › Reports

  1. Choose a Period: Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, This month, Last month, This quarter or Custom (pick From and To). Choose a Granularity for the charts. Every number is compared with the period before (▲▼).
  2. Narrow the numbers by Queue, Tier, Priority, Kind, Channel, Label or Agent. Clear filters resets them. The address of the page carries your choices, so you can share it.
  3. Tick Business hours only to count first response, resolution, chat wait and reply times only inside each queue’s business hours.
  4. Read the panels (below) and click a bar to see its cases. CSV downloads a table. Refresh reloads.
  • Numbers: cases created, resolved, closed and reopened, the backlog, Breaches, First response median, First-response SLA met, Resolution SLA met, Chat wait median, Chats abandoned, Chats converted, Reply time median, messages received and replies sent.
  • Panels: Volume (broken down by queue, tier, priority or label), Channels, Response times (median and p90), SLA attainment, First response time distribution, Sources and labels, Traffic by day and hour (a heatmap) and Agents (assigned, first response and resolution medians, reopen rate, open now, messages sent).
  • AI assistant: what the assistant resolved by itself, what it handled with help, what it Handed to a person and why (Why the AI handed over), the Handoff rate, Questions not answered, and for handed-over chats the median wait and the rating.
  • CSAT: see ratings.
  • Year in review (a button at the top) sums up your year and the team’s: resolved, replies sent, median first response, busiest month and day, top label.

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: any
  1. In Settings › Queues, tick Ask for a satisfaction rating (CSAT) on a queue.
  2. When a case of that queue is resolved, the customer gets one survey: a chat in the chat window, a ticket by email.

Help center

A help center is a public website of help articles, such as “How do I track my order?”. Shoppers can also read the articles in the chat window under Help articles.

How do I start a help center?

  • Who: Owner, Admin (or a custom role with Manage the help center)
  • Plan: any

Codexta › Support console › Help center

  1. Add a portal (New portal) with a name, languages and colours.
  2. Write articles in Articles, or use Draft from website to draft them from your site. Everything starts as a draft; press Publish when ready.
  3. Suggested FAQs: the AI reads resolved cases and suggests questions customers keep asking, with your team’s answers.
  4. The site is at /hc/your-portal. You can point your own domain at it.

In a reply, agents can insert a link to an article. Add to knowledge base lets the assistant answer from an article too.

Example: write and publish an article

  • Who: Owner, Admin (or a custom role with Manage the help center)

Codexta › Support console › Help center › Articles

  1. Press New. Fill in Title (for example How do I track my order?), the Category, Short description (shown in lists) and the Article text. The text is Markdown: # heading, **bold**, *italic*, - lists, [link](https://…) and ![image](https://…). Add an image uploads one.
  2. Address (from the title) is the web address of the article. Tick Featured on the home page to pin it. Under Search engines you can set a Meta title and Meta description.
  3. Press Save as draft. Edits are a draft until you publish them. Discard changes drops them.
  4. Press Publish. View opens it on the site. Archive takes it offline and Restore brings it back as a draft.
  5. Translate with AI into… makes a draft in another language of the portal. The article’s Translations list them.

The article list filters by All, Drafts, Published and Archived and searches with Search articles. Tick several to Publish, Archive, Feature, Unfeature, Move to category… or translate. Each article shows its views and unpublished changes.

How do I organise articles into categories?

  • Who: Owner, Admin (or a custom role with Manage the help center)

Codexta › Support console › Help center › Categories

Press Add category and fill in Category name, Address (from the name), Icon (an emoji), Order, Parent category (Top level or inside another), Translation key (same in every language) and Description. Deleting a category keeps its articles, without a category.

What can I set on the portal?

  • Who: Owner, Admin (or a custom role with Manage the help center)

Codexta › Support console › Help center › Portal settings

  • Name and Address (the site is at /hc/your-portal), Header text (How can we help?) and Page title.
  • Languages (comma-separated, such as en,bs,de), Default language and Texts per language for the name, header and title.
  • Brand color, Layout (Cards or Docs (categories in a sidebar)), Logo URL, Website and Social links (one per line, such as Instagram https://instagram.com/shop).
  • Chat widget channel and Widget shop id put the chat on the site (empty: no chat). Under Analytics on the site you can enter ids for Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics 4, Plausible, Hotjar, Microsoft Clarity, Meta Pixel and Amplitude.
  • Own domain: point your domain at the site with a CNAME record, save, then press Check. Until it is checked the site stays at /hc/your-portal.
  • Archived (the site is offline) switches the portal off. Delete removes it with all its articles.

How do Suggested FAQs work?

  • Who: Owner, Admin (or a custom role with Manage the help center)

Codexta › Support console › Help center › Suggested FAQs

  1. Press Find suggestions. The AI reads resolved cases and suggests questions customers keep asking, with the answers your team gave. Each shows in how many cases it was asked.
  2. Filter by Status: To review, Approved or Dismissed.
  3. For a suggestion, press Add to knowledge base (the assistant can then answer it), Draft an article (saved as a draft article) or Dismiss. Tick several to act on them together.

Knowledge coverage (30 days) shows Notes used, Searches and Questions not answered, so you can see where your notes fall short.

Campaigns, monitors and calls

Campaigns

  • Who: Owner, Admin

Settings › Campaigns: a Widget message on your pages after a shopper spends some seconds on a page you choose, or a One-off SMS to contacts with a label (needs an SMS number). Steps: the setup guide.

Monitors

  • Who: Owner, Admin

Settings › Monitors: describe in plain English what to look for, for example “the customer says they will cancel or go to a competitor”. The AI checks every resolved case and lists the matches with its reason. It can tag them. Preview on recent cases tries it first. Steps: the setup guide.

Calls

  • Who: Owner, Admin

With an SMS number connected in Codexta’s Settings › Integrations, the same number can take phone calls (Settings › Calls). In business hours your team’s phones ring; otherwise the caller leaves a voicemail. Calls are logged on the caller’s case and can be recorded and transcribed. The Calls section of the console lists them with their status, length, agent, recording and case. Every field: the setup guide.

For developers: bots, webhooks, apps

Most shops do not need these. They are for a developer working with you.

  • Agent bots: your own bot takes a channel’s new cases first; a person replying takes over. Not on every plan.
  • Webhooks: a signed message to your server when a case is created, changed, resolved or reopened, a message is added or a satisfaction rating arrives.
  • Dashboard apps: your own web page as a tab beside every case.
  • An API channel: your own app sends conversations into the helpdesk.

Steps for each: webhooks, agent bots, dashboard apps, the API channel.

Customer data and the audit log

How do I erase a customer’s data?

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: any

Codexta › Support console › Settings › Data & audit

  1. Under Erase a customer’s data, enter the Customer email to erase and press Erase.
  2. Confirm. Every case of that customer is deleted, and what they wrote on other cases is blanked. This cannot be undone.

When your shop platform sends a deletion request for a customer, this happens by itself.

How do I see who changed what?

The Audit log on the same tab lists configuration changes, deleted cases, blocked contacts and erased data, with who did it and when. You can search it, filter by type, object and date, and show the oldest first. See the setup guide.

Can I bring my old helpdesk over?

Import from another helpdesk brings contacts and conversations, with their messages, notes and tags, from Freshdesk or Intercom. Running it again skips what was already imported.
