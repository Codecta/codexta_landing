Codexta for WooCommerce

Upload the plugin and connect your shop; a free 7-day trial starts at once. Then teach the assistant about your shop.

Install

  1. In WordPress, open Plugins › Add New Plugin › Upload Plugin, choose the .zip file and press Install Now.

  2. Press Activate Plugin.

  3. Open WooCommerce › Codexta Assistant and press Connect to Codexta.

  4. Approve access on the WooCommerce screen. You come back to a page saying Connected to Codexta.

  5. Your free 7-day trial starts now. Contact us to agree a plan before it ends; when it has ended you see Waiting for activation by Codexta until Codexta activates your plan.

  6. Open Overview, press Set up my assistant, then check the chat button on your shop.

Tip. You need WordPress 6.4, PHP 7.4 and WooCommerce 8.0 or newer. There are no keys or passwords to copy.

Then teach your assistant

The same in every shop. Start with the first two.

  • Name, greeting and questionsWhat shoppers see when the chat opens.
  • Add an articleWhat your assistant knows: delivery, returns, sizes.
  • Import a web page or PDFTurn pages and files you already have into articles.
  • Add a lessonHow your assistant behaves: a short nudge.
  • LanguagesThe chat’s language and your shoppers’.
  • Chat on your storefrontSee, move or hide the chat button.

Choose which coupons the assistant can offer

The assistant only offers a coupon you have marked for it. Unmarked coupons stay private, so internal codes are safe.

  1. Open WooCommerce › Marketing › Coupons and open the coupon.

  2. Add #aiassistant to its Description, for example Spring sale, 10% off #aiassistant, and press Update.

  3. Ask the assistant “Is there a discount?” to check. Shoppers never see the mark.

Tip. A marked coupon is still not offered when it is a draft, limited to certain emails, expired or used up. To stop offering one, remove the mark.

Choose the pages that show the chat

The chat button is on every page by default. To keep it off some pages, open Settings › General › Pages with the chat, type one pattern per line and press Save settings. Leave it empty to show the chat everywhere.

  • ^/$The home page only.
  • ^/products/Every product page.
  • ^/(?!cart|checkout)Everywhere except the cart and checkout.

Tip. Put every exclusion in one line, as above: two separate lines would each let the other page through. Write paths as they appear in your address bar. More examples are in the full reference.

Deactivate, disconnect and your data

Deactivating the plugin pauses the assistant: the chat button disappears and nothing is deleted. Activate it again to resume.

Disconnecting (the Disconnect button on WooCommerce › Codexta Assistant) or deleting the plugin removes the access key and webhooks Codexta used. Your shop’s data in Codexta is deleted 48 hours later; connect again before then and everything is still there. See the privacy policy.
