Channels and email

A channel is a way cases come in. Email is the most common.

Open it
  • Shopify Shopify admin › Apps › Codexta › Support console
  • WooCommerce WooCommerce › Codexta Assistant › Support console
  • Other systems The admin link in your Codexta welcome email, then Support console in the top menu

  1. Open Settings › Channels and press Add channel.

  2. Choose the Kind: Email, Live chat or API. You cannot change it later.

  3. Type a Code, a Name and, for email, your public Address.

  4. Pick the Queue where new cases should land, then press Save.

  5. To send replies from your own mail server, open Settings › Email, fill in the server and press Test.

Tip. Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram and other messaging apps connect under Settings › Integrations in the Codexta admin, not here.
