Docs›Team and plan›Team and roles

Add colleagues and set roles

Pick a role for each person. The Owner decides.

Open it
  • Shopify Shopify admin › Apps › Codexta
  • WooCommerce WooCommerce › Codexta Assistant
  • Other systems The admin link in your Codexta welcome email

  1. Shopify: the colleague must be staff in your Shopify admin. WooCommerce: give them the Shop manager role in WordPress. Other systems: ask your Codexta contact to give them access.

  2. Ask them to open Codexta once.

  3. Open Settings › Team and find them under Members.

  4. Choose their role in the menu. You see Role saved. Press Remove to take access away.

RoleCan do
OwnerEverything, including roles and plan.
AdminEverything except changing roles and plan.
KB editorEdit the knowledge base.
Support managerCases and reports.
Support agentAnswer cases.
ViewerRead conversations.

Tip. Only the Owner changes roles. Nobody can change their own role, and the last Owner cannot be removed.
