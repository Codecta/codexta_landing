Docs›Support console›Live chat hours

Set your live chat hours

Outside these hours the assistant takes a ticket instead.

Open it
  • Shopify Shopify admin › Apps › Codexta › Support console
  • WooCommerce WooCommerce › Codexta Assistant › Support console
  • Other systems The admin link in your Codexta welcome email, then Support console in the top menu

  1. Open Settings › Backends and check that Live chat with a person is on.

  2. Open Settings › Live chat and choose Always open, Follow business hours or Off.

  3. To pause for a while, press 30 minutes, 1 hour or Until tomorrow.

  4. Change the opening times under Settings › Business hours.

Tip. Each change saves as you make it. A chat that is already open is never cut off. A pause ends by itself.
