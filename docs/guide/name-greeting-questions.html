Set the name, greeting and questions

Make the first thing a customer sees sound like you.

Open it
  • Shopify Shopify admin › Apps › Codexta
  • WooCommerce WooCommerce › Codexta Assistant
  • Other systems The admin link in your Codexta welcome email

  1. Open Settings in the top menu, then the General tab.

  2. Type a name in Assistant name. Pick an Accent colour to match your brand.

  3. Under Greeting and suggested questions, write your Greeting.

  4. Fill Idea 1 to Idea 4 with questions customers can tap, such as “How do I contact you?”.

  5. Press Save settings. You see Saved.

Tip. Prefer to chat? Open Assistant › Chat and type “Change the greeting and starter questions”.
