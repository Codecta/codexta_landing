Add an article

The assistant answers from your articles. What is not written there, it does not know.

Open it
  • Shopify Shopify admin › Apps › Codexta
  • WooCommerce WooCommerce › Codexta Assistant
  • Other systems The admin link in your Codexta welcome email

  1. Open Knowledge base in the top menu.

  2. Press Add knowledge and pick Write an article.

  3. Type a Title, such as “Returns and refunds”, and the Text. Write it as you would tell a new colleague.

  4. Press Add. The article appears on the Articles tab.

Tip. Headings such as ## Shipping costs help the assistant find the right part of a long article. One topic per article works best.
