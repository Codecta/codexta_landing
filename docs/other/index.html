Other systems

Codexta runs inside any application that has its own users, set up as a standalone custom account.

Banking and insurance portals, ERP systems and bespoke applications are not installed from an app store. Codexta creates a standalone (custom) account for your application. Your developer adds the chat to your pages and a small page that tells Codexta who is signed in.

  • Connect a custom applicationWhat Codexta needs from you, and the steps from first contact to first chat.
  • Signed user tokenFor your developer: sign the token that identifies the signed-in user.

Tip. Running a shop on Shopify, WooCommerce, Shopware or BigCommerce? Use Online shops instead.
