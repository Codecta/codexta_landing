02

Step by step

Steps 1 to 4 take you from install to a working assistant. The rest are the settings most shops adjust next. Places are written as Shopify admin › Apps › Codexta › Settings › Team.

1. Install Codexta

  • Who: the Shopify account owner
  • Plan: any
  1. Install Codexta from the Shopify App Store and approve the access Shopify asks for.
  2. Shopify opens Codexta in your admin. You become the Owner.

Details: How do I install Codexta?.

2. Choose a plan

  • Who: Owner
  • Plan: any

Shopify admin › Apps › Codexta › Settings › Plan

  1. Press Choose a plan in the banner, or let Codexta send you to Shopify’s plan page.
  2. Pick a plan and approve it in Shopify. The free trial, if shown there, starts when you approve.

Until you do, the assistant does not answer shoppers.

Details: How do I choose a plan and start the trial?.

3. Turn the storefront assistant on

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: any

Shopify admin › Apps › Codexta › Overview › Set up your assistant

  1. Under Show the assistant on your storefront., press Open the theme editor.
  2. Check that Codexta assistant is switched on under App embeds.
  3. Press Save.

You can switch it off again the same way.

Details: How do I show the assistant on my storefront?.

4. Run the guided setup

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: any

Shopify admin › Apps › Codexta › Overview › Set up your assistant

  1. Press Set up my assistant. Codexta opens Assistant › Chat and sends “Set up my assistant” for you.
  2. Answer its questions. It asks which languages your shoppers write in, drafts a name, a greeting and up to four starter questions, and starts the knowledge base from your Shopify policies and online-store pages.
  3. Check the exact text it shows. Nothing is saved before you approve it.
  4. In the Test step, ask a few real questions. Try questions about orders, stock, prices or the cart on your storefront.

You can skip an area or stop and come back later. Type “What is left to set up” to continue.

Details: How do I run the guided setup?.

5. Add to the knowledge base

  • Who: Owner, Admin, KB editor
  • Plan: any

Shopify admin › Apps › Codexta › Knowledge base

  1. Press New note, enter a path such as faq/shipping.md and press Create.
  2. Write the note and press Save.
  3. To add a web page or a PDF, use Add document (web page or PDF).
  4. Or ask in Assistant › Chat: “Add my shipping and returns pages to the knowledge base”.

Details: How do I add a note?.

6. Set the name, greeting, starter questions and languages

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: any

Shopify admin › Apps › Codexta › Settings › General

  1. Change them in the chat (“Change the greeting and starter questions”) or in Settings › General under Assistant name and Greeting and suggested questions.
  2. To add a language, choose it from + Add language and fill in its tab.
  3. Press Save settings.

Details: Name, greeting, starter questions and languages.

7. Choose which pages show the chat

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: any

Shopify admin › Apps › Codexta › Settings › General › Pages with the chat

  1. By default the chat button is on every page. To limit it, type one regular expression per line.
  2. Leave the box empty, or enter *, to show it everywhere.
  3. Press Save settings.

Details: How do I choose the pages that show the chat?.

8. Decide who can chat and how much

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: any

Shopify admin › Apps › Codexta › Settings › General › Who can chat

  1. Untick Allow guests to let only signed-in shoppers chat.
  2. Optionally set Conversations per shopper per day and Guests’ share of monthly conversations (%).
  3. Press Save settings.

Guests can ask what your knowledge base answers, search products and use the cart. Their own orders and returns need signing in.

Details: Guest shoppers and chat limits.

9. Take over a chat in the support console

  • Who: Owner, Admin, Support manager, Support agent
  • Plan: any (live chat switched on)

Shopify admin › Apps › Codexta › Support console › Inbox

  1. Switch Live chat with a person on under Support console › Settings › Backends.
  2. When a shopper asks for a person, open their chat under Unassigned and press Assign to me.
  3. Write in Reply. The shopper sees Connected to a human agent.
  4. Press Resolve when done.

Details: How do I take over a chat from the assistant?.

10. Add WhatsApp, Viber and phone buttons

  • Who: Owner, Admin
  • Plan: any

Shopify admin › Apps › Codexta › Settings › General › Contact options

  1. Enter WhatsApp number, Viber number or Phone number (calls) in international format, such as +387 61 123 456.
  2. Press Save settings.

They become Chat on WhatsApp, Chat on Viber and Call us buttons when the assistant cannot answer.

Details: Contact options in the support console guide.

11. Give your team roles

  • Who: Owner
  • Plan: any

Shopify admin › Apps › Codexta › Settings › Team

  1. Ask your colleague to open Codexta once from the Shopify admin. They appear as Viewer.
  2. Under Members, choose their role in the dropdown.

There is no invite button: colleagues must be staff in your Shopify admin.

Details: How do I give a colleague a role?.

12. Uninstall and your data

  • Who: Shopify account owner
  • Plan: any
  1. Uninstalling cancels your Shopify subscription and revokes Codexta’s access to your shop.
  2. 48 hours after you uninstall, all of your shop’s data is deleted. If you reinstall before then, your setup is reactivated and you choose a plan again.

Details: Uninstall and your data.

03

What the app asks permission for and why

When you install, Shopify shows the access Codexta asks for and you approve it. In plain words:

  • Products. The assistant reads your products so it can answer shoppers’ questions, search and compare products, and help fill the cart. In the back office it can also change a product, a price or a collection, but only after you say yes to a question naming the old and the new value.
  • Customers and orders, for signed-in shoppers. A signed-in shopper can ask about their own orders and request a return. A guest is not identified beyond an anonymous session.
  • Customers and orders, for your team. The back-office assistant can look up customers and orders when you or an Admin ask, for example “Show me order #1004”. Each lookup is recorded in a customer data access log.
  • Payments. Payments never pass through Codexta. Checkout always happens in Shopify.

Read how the data is handled in the Security and data protection page (including the Shopify protected customer data section), the Data Processing Addendum and the privacy policy. Uninstalling revokes the access; see Uninstall and your data.
