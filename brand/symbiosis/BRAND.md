# Codexta symbiosis identity

## Idea

The mark represents human and artificial intelligence working together around a shared, verified outcome.

- Teal organic half: human context, judgment, and empathy.
- Violet geometric half: artificial intelligence, systems, and execution.
- Coral center: the decision or completed outcome shared by both.

## Primary files

- `codexta-logo-light.svg`: default horizontal logo on white or light surfaces.
- `codexta-logo-dark.svg`: horizontal logo on dark surfaces.
- `codexta-logo-stacked.svg`: square or portrait placements.
- `codexta-mark.svg`: favicon, app icon, and small placements.
- `codexta-social-avatar.svg`: social profile image.

| AI violet | `#6757E5` | Geometric half |
| Shared coral | `#F97066` | Center outcome only |
| Codexta navy | `#101828` | Primary text and dark field |
| Secondary gray | `#667085` | Endorsement text |

## Usage

- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least half the mark's height.
- Use the complete horizontal logo at widths of 220px or greater.
- Below 220px, use the mark without the endorsement.
- Below 32px, interior details may simplify naturally; preserve the two-color split and coral center.
- Do not recolor individual halves, rotate the mark, add effects, or place it on a visually busy image.
- Preserve the wording and capitalization: `Codexta` and `developed by codecta.com`.
