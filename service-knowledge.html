KNOWLEDGE · A RARE PROCEDURE

Lena handles something she sees twice a year.

Lena works in a bank’s back office. A bereavement case is rare, sensitive and easy to get wrong: the steps are in a long manual, and one rule changed last month.

TUESDAY · BACK OFFICE

A customer’s father has died. He had a joint current account with her mother and a savings account in his own name. What do I need to do?
THE NEWEST RULE WINS

The operations manual says up to £30,000 can be released without probate. A memo from last month raised it to £50,000. Codexta reads the procedure, the form checklist and the memo, and uses the current limit.

C
Codexta
Here are the current steps, combined from the procedure, the form checklist and last month’s memo.
KNOWLEDGE BASEBereavement — joint current account and sole savings account

Current rule: last month’s memo raised the amount we can release without probate from £30,000 to £50,000. The operations manual still shows £30,000.

  1. Joint current account: it passes to the mother. Remove the father’s name; don’t freeze or close it.
  2. Savings account in his name: freeze it until the estate is settled.
  3. Ask for the death certificate and ID of the person handling the estate.
  4. Up to £50,000: release with form BER-2 and a signed indemnity. Above: ask for probate.
  5. Send the family the bereavement letter with the direct bereavement line.

WHY IT MATTERS

Without Codexta, Lena searches a long manual, may follow the outdated limit, and asks a colleague to be sure. With it, she gets the current steps in seconds and can open each source to check.

HOW A PROBLEM IS HANDLED

Every problem makes the next one easier.

Codexta follows the same steps every time. Your approved content comes first, people step in when it isn’t enough, and what they learn is kept.

01 / ANSWER

Approved content first.

The customer describes the problem in their own words. Codexta checks your knowledge base and offers what matches.

02 / HAND OVER

A person when nothing fits.

The customer confirms whether it helped. If not, a colleague joins the same chat, or a ticket reaches your team, with everything already explained.

03 / RESOLVE

Support finds the fix.

The agent works the case in the support console and records the resolution. Hard cases can move to a specialist tier or a named colleague.

04 / LEARN

The fix is kept, with approval.

Codexta drafts a general note without personal details. Someone with permission approves it before it is added.
